House rules

  1. Wake time is the anchor. Seven days a week, even after a party.
  2. Hardest task first. Low on Oedipus? Write the thesis. Just the thesis.
  3. No caffeine after 1pm. Naps ≤20 min, before 3pm.
  4. Screens off 45 min before lights out. Bed only when sleepy.
  5. One guilt-free half day a week. Family time counts.
  6. Sunday 5pm review before English tutoring: set next week's high and low days.
  7. Motivation flat for over a week? Tell a parent, your tutor or the school counsellor.
